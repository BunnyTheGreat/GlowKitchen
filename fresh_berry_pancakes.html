Fresh Berry Pancakes


Pancakes are a beloved breakfast classic that have been enjoyed by generations. Whether you like them fluffy, stacked high, or filled with fresh berries, there's no denying the deliciousness of a well-made pancake. In this blog post, I'll share my favorite recipe for fresh berry pancakes, along with some tips and tricks to make them just right.

Every Saturday morning, I find myself craving the sweet aroma of pancakes on the griddle. This recipe is particularly special to me because it combines my love for fresh berries with the comfort of a traditional pancake. The key to these pancakes is the perfect balance of tartness from the berries and the sweetness of the batter.

I love to experiment with different combinations of berries, depending on what's in season. Blueberries, raspberries, and strawberries all work wonderfully in this recipe, each adding their unique flavor and color to the pancakes. Don't be afraid to mix and match, or even throw in some chocolate chips if you're feeling indulgent!

Flare Friendly: This recipe is considered 'ok' on the flare friendly meter for most people based on their experience. Due to a higher ratio of empty carbohydrates compared to the amount of fiber, fat, and protein, it may be a trigger for eczema flare-ups for SOME people. To make this a more flare-friendly meal, try pairing these pancakes with your favorite yogurt and nut butter. You can even mix in protein powder into the batter for a more dense meal. No matter how you choose to enjoy this recipe, please remember to indulge in these delectable, fresh pancakes 100% guilt free!

Prep Time: 10 mins

Cook Time: 20 mins

Total Time: 30 mins

Yield: 4 servings

Save to Pinterest

Ingredients

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1 egg
  • 1 cup buttermilk
  • 2 tablespoons melted butter
  • 1 cup fresh blueberries

Instructions

  1. Step 1: In a large bowl, whisk together flour, sugar, baking powder, baking soda, and salt.
  2. Step 2: In another bowl, beat the egg and then whisk in buttermilk and melted butter.
  3. Step 3: Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients and stir until just combined. Fold in blueberries.
  4. Step 4: Heat a non-stick pan over medium heat and pour 1/4 cup of batter for each pancake. Cook until bubbles form, flip, and cook until golden brown.
